Caveat: moving a job only saves CO2 if the cluster actually uses less power when the job is not running (auto-scaling or pause). dp-prod and dp-dev currently run around the clock, so the saving only becomes real together with a schedule or auto-pause measure.

Protection windows: on dp-prod, jobs keep their current start on the last 3 working days of each month and at year-end close (finance closing, T-1029), so nothing runs alongside the month-end close. dp-dev keeps quarterly training free on days 1–3 of Jan, Apr, Jul and Oct (T-1053). "Best start" is the most frequent best hour across months; the CO2 change uses the best hour of each month.
